---
title: From Nothing
date: 2026-05-22
description: What scarcity teaches that abundance never can.
---

Nobody handed me a network. I built one transaction at a time.

When you start from nothing, you learn two things early. The first is that no one is coming — not to rescue you, not to discover you, not to give you permission. The second is that this is good news, because if no one is coming, no one can stop you either.

Constraint is underrated. When you can't buy your way through a problem, you have to think your way through it, and thinking compounds. The overnight queues, the self-built bot, the cold message that actually got answered — none of it came from resources. It came from wanting the outcome badly enough to find the seam nobody else bothered to look for.

I don't romanticize the hard parts. They were just hard. But scarcity taught me a resourcefulness that abundance never could, and I'd take that trade every time.

You can start from nothing. Nothing is a fine place to start.
